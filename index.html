About

Built by Ememart Solutions

Zed is developed and operated by Ememart Solutions, based in Nnewi, Anambra State, Nigeria. We build practical, mobile-first tools for shop owners who need to manage stock, suppliers, and staff without a full back-office setup.

What Zed does

Everything a shop needs to track, in one app

No logins are required to view this page — Zed's own dashboard is login-protected for staff, since it holds each shop's stock and sales data.

01

Inventory & batch tracking

Track stock by batch, with expiry dates and low-stock alerts, so nothing gets sold past its date or forgotten in the back room.

02

Supplier & restock logging

Log purchases from suppliers, record what's been paid and what's still owed, and see a full purchase history per supplier.

03

Multi-user staff access

Give staff their own PIN-based logins with role-based permissions, so owners keep control while staff can still ring up sales.

04

Multi-shop support

Manage more than one shop location from the same account, switching between them without losing track of stock.
