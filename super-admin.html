Platform Pricing

Your margin, per service

Applied to Bigisub's real cost on every purchase, platform-wide — invisible to businesses; they're simply charged the final amount. There is no reseller/markup pricing on the business side at all, so this is the only margin the platform earns. A single blanket percentage doesn't work for every service — funding a ₦50,000 betting wallet at 2% would add ₦1,000, which isn't how a betting funding fee should work — so each service below has its own rule, in whichever unit actually fits it: a percentage for services with variable cost (data, airtime, cable, etc.), or a flat ₦ fee for pass-through services where the exact amount matters (betting funding, electricity tokens).
